About Drip

Drip is a simple, private subscription tracker for iPhone. Add your subscriptions once and see what you really spend each month and each year, with reminders before every renewal and before a free trial starts charging.

Everything lives on your device. There is no account, no bank login, and no sign-up.

Support

Help & FAQ

How do I add a subscription?

Tap the + button on the main screen, enter the name, price, and billing cycle, then save. Drip does the monthly and yearly math for you.

How do renewal reminders work?

Drip can notify you a few days before each renewal. For a free trial, it reminds you to cancel before the trial ends and the charge begins. You choose how many days ahead in the editor.

What is the difference between Free and Premium?

Free tracks up to 5 subscriptions with full reminders and trial tracking. Drip Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited subscriptions, spending by category, a yearly spending breakdown, and monthly budget alerts. There is no recurring fee.

I bought Premium on another device. How do I get it back?

Open the menu and tap Restore Purchases. Your one-time purchase is tied to your Apple Account.

How do I delete my data?

Delete any subscription by opening it and choosing delete. Removing the app from your iPhone erases all Drip data, since nothing is stored anywhere else.

Need more help?

Visit the Drip page on the App Store and use the developer support options there.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Drip is built to be private by design.

  • No data collected. Drip does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • On device only. The subscriptions and settings you enter are stored on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to any server owned by us.
  • No accounts, no tracking. Drip has no sign-up and includes no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device to remind you about renewals and trials.
  • Purchases. Drip Premium is handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

Because Drip stores nothing on our side, there is no personal data for us to access, change, or delete. Removing the app removes your data.

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
